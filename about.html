Who We Are

Dedicated to Excellence in Technical Education

NRI Institute of Technology (NRIIT) is committed to providing world-class, industry-aligned education in Python FullStack Engineering and Web Technologies.

Our LMS portal bridges academia and industry expectations by giving engineering students continuous access to hands-on coding exercises, real-time feedback, interactive modules, and direct mentorship from experienced trainers.
