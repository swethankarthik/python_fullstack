Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding NRIIT LMS training programs.

What are the eligibility criteria for the Python FullStack track?
Anyone with basic computer operational skills can join. No prior coding experience is mandatory, as the curriculum starts from HTML5, CSS3, and foundational Python.
Is placement assistance guaranteed after course completion?
Yes! Our dedicated Placement Cell provides resume building, mock technical interviews, profile optimization, and direct referral drives with 100+ hiring partners.
Can I attend classes online or hybrid?
We offer both on-campus classroom sessions at Agiripalli and live interactive online sessions with recorded access on the student portal.
Will I receive an official University certificate?
Yes, upon successful completion of the course track and capstone project, students receive an official Dr. RVR & NRI Institute of Technology Certificate.
